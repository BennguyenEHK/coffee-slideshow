It starts as a fruit

Coffee grows as a small red cherry. Inside are usually two seeds, and those seeds are the beans.

Legend says an Ethiopian goat herder named Kaldi noticed his goats getting lively after eating the cherries.

Two species run the market

Arabica

94.4million bags, 2025/26

Sweeter and more acidic. Usually grown higher up. Roughly 1.2–1.5% caffeine.

Robusta

84.4million bags, 2025/26

Bolder and more bitter. A hardier plant. Roughly 2.2–2.7% caffeine.

Heat turns green to brown

Three numbers to get right

90–96°C

Water temperature for brewing (195–205°F). Hotter tastes bitter, cooler tastes sour.

55 g

Coffee per litre of water, the classic starting ratio for filter coffee.

9 bar

The usual pressure behind a shot of espresso.
